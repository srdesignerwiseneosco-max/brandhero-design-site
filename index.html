Who we are

Built to protect brands from mediocrity.

A boutique design consultancy that works on the principle of Design that Works. Brandhero brings together storytelling, graphic design, experience design and technology to create unique brand and customer experiences.

Brandhero is more than a design studio — the creative partner brands call when they need to evolve, grow, and lead their category. Every decision is built around making the business stand out: increasing conversion, strengthening perception, improving engagement, creating lasting brand recall, and building trust faster.

Headquartered in Bengaluru (WeWork), with presence noted in BLR, India and NY, USA. Founded in 2016 by Vinamra Agrawal. Specialisations include branding, brand identity, UX/UI, Webflow, Framer, marketing websites, and no-code.

You already paid for the traffic

Ads, SEO, and outbound only work if the site converts. Vague headlines, confusing nav, and generic pages waste CAC. Brandhero’s philosophy: make the post-click experience work harder with clearer positioning, stronger UX, and better storytelling.

LinkedIn · AI + humans

Not every task needs AI — or a human

Use AI for speed, repetition, and scale (research, sorting, first drafts, design variations). Keep humans for brand strategy, creative direction, crisis calls, and high-stakes decisions. AI should make teams faster — not thinking weaker.

LinkedIn · Automation

Small teams need better automation

If a 10-person team still manually chases leads, follow-ups, meetings, and weekly reports, the fix may be workflow automation — not more headcount. Remove repetitive work so people focus on strategy, sales, and creativity.

LinkedIn · UI trends

Beyond the Bento Grid trend

Bento layouts are everywhere on SaaS sites — but a popular pattern isn’t a strategy. If removing the logo makes the site look like a competitor’s, the real need is message, journey, and brand strategy — not another trendy component.

LinkedIn · Brand systems

Identity that moves, not sits still

Instagram’s identity shift toward motion and flexible systems shows modern brands must work across Reels, Stories, and messages — not only a static guidelines PDF. Ask: is your identity designed to sit still, or designed to live?

LinkedIn · Community brands

Build believers, not just products

Fast-growing brands win when customers feel they belong — not only because the product is better. Strong identity and community marketing give people a reason to identify with the brand, not just buy from it.

LinkedIn · Consistency

Memorable identity beats a clever logo

Investors can like a product and forget the brand in minutes if nothing is memorable. Trust comes from a consistent visual system — fonts, colors, personality — repeated until recognition is automatic.

Founder post · Homepage clarity

Your homepage is burning cash

In the first five seconds a stranger should know what you do, who it’s for, and why they should care. If not, paid traffic bounces — that’s CAC leakage. Fix clarity, hierarchy, and CTA; stop decorating and start designing revenue.
